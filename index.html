Quick Guide

How to use ICCOP Lookup

Search

Enter a facility code/name and/or a drug name (generic, trade, or HCPC), then Look Up. One field alone still works.

Verdict colors

OptumRx at risk — reviews & supplies
Split — review and supply go to different sides
Not at risk — medical group handles it, or not covered
Unclear — not found, escalate before denying

Results & verbiage

Facility shows its list assignments; Drug List Matches shows every list the drug is on (gold badge = the match). Fill both fields to get copy-ready note/verbiage blocks.

Updating data

Upload updated Job Aid (.xlsx) parses a new workbook right in your browser — nothing leaves your machine. Reset reverts to the bundled version.
